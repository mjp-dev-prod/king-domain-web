King Domain

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

King Domain helps students and emerging talent prove their skills, get hired and get paid, and helps clients hire them. To do that we handle some of your personal information. This page says what, why, who else handles it, and how you can have it deleted.

Who we are

King Domain is run by MJP Productions Limited ("we", "us"), a company registered in Nigeria. We decide how and why your personal data is used, which makes us the "data controller" under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. To contact us about your data, write to kingdomainapp@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

What we do not do. We do not sell your data. We do not show advertising. The app does not include advertising or analytics tools, and does not ask for your location, contacts or microphone. It reads your photos or camera only when you choose to add a profile photo or proof of work.

To draw its text in our chosen typefaces, the app downloads font files from Google Fonts. Google can therefore see that your phone made that request, along with its internet address.

Who can see what

  • Your profile photo is public. Anyone who has its web address can open it, inside or outside the app. Do not use a picture you would not want others to see. You can remove it at any time.
  • Students who look at a job can see the client's name, photo and email address.
  • Clients who receive an application can see the student's name, photo, email address, headline, how many jobs they have completed, and up to three of their verified proof-of-work files in that job's category.
  • Proof-of-work and delivered files are not public. They sit in private storage and open only through links that stop working after a few minutes. Besides you, our reviewers can open your proof, a client weighing your application can open the verified proof described above, and the client on a job can open what you delivered for it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile, proof of work, payout details and sign-in records are kept while your account exists, and removed when you delete it.
  • Records of finished jobs and payments are kept after you delete your account, without your name, for as long as the law requires us to keep financial records. The person on the other side of that job still needs them to show their own work history.
  • Paystack keeps its own transaction records under its own rules. We cannot erase those.
  • After deletion, copies can remain in our providers' backups until those are overwritten.
  • A waitlist sign-up is kept until you ask us to remove it, or until we have told you King Domain is open.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, have mistakes corrected, have your data deleted, limit or object to how we use it, receive a copy you can take elsewhere, and withdraw any consent you gave. Some of this you can do yourself in the app (edit your profile, remove your photo, delete your account). For the rest, email kingdomainapp@gmail.com from the address on your account so we know it is you.

If you think we have handled your data wrongly and we have not put it right, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission at ndpc.gov.ng.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile, then Delete account, and enter your password. This removes your profile, photo, proof of work, payout details and sign-in details, as described above. You cannot delete your account while a job is in progress, because the other person is relying on you. Finish it, or wait for it to be resolved, and then try again.

If you cannot use the app, see how to ask us to delete your account.

Keeping your data safe

Data travels over encrypted connections. Passwords are stored in a form that cannot be turned back into the password. Proof-of-work and delivered files sit in private storage. If a breach is likely to put you at risk, we will tell the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of finding out, and tell you without undue delay.

Changes to this page

When we change this policy we change the date at the top. If a change affects how we use your data in a way you would not expect, we will also tell you in the app or by email.
