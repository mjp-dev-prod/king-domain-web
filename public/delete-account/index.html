King Domain

Delete your account

You can delete your King Domain account, and the personal data attached to it, in the app or by email. This page is run by MJP Productions Limited, who operate King Domain.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open King Domain and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account, enter your password, and confirm.

It takes effect straight away, and we email you to confirm.

If you cannot use the app

Email kingdomainapp@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my King Domain account". We use that address to check it is you. We will delete the account and email you when it is done, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in details and every signed-in device.
  • Your profile, photo and bio, your proof of work and its files, and your saved payout details.
  • Applications that were not chosen, and any job you posted that nobody was hired for.
  • Your waitlist sign-up, if you made one with the same email address.

What is kept, and why

Finished jobs and their payment records are kept without your name, for as long as the law requires us to keep financial records. The person on the other side of the job needs them to show their own work history.

Paystack, our payment provider, keeps its own transaction records under its own rules. Copies of deleted data can also remain in our providers' backups until they are overwritten.

If you have a job in progress

We cannot delete an account while a job is in progress, because the other person is relying on you. Finish the job, or wait until it has been resolved, and then delete your account. If you ask us by email while a job is in progress, we will tell you which job is holding it up.

More detail is in our privacy policy.
